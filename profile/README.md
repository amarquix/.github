# Amarquix

**One platform. Your whole business.**

Amarquix is an AI software development and managed platform agency. We design and build End-to-End Custom AI Systems around how growing businesses actually operate.

Instead of relying on generic SaaS tools or fragile automations, we unify scattered tools, daily workflows, and private AI into one central platform built with custom software.

## What We Do

- **End-to-End Custom AI Systems:** Custom software, code-based task automation, and practical private AI (LLMs, document search, and voice workflows) unified into a single platform.
- **Managed Cloud Infrastructure:** Dedicated servers, LLM and Speech AI APIs, GPU compute, object storage, and business email hosted on Amarquix Cloud.
- **Employee Training & Onboarding:** Hands-on, role-based walkthroughs and plain-English documentation for smooth team adoption.
- **Maintenance & 24/7 Support:** Continuous monitoring, security updates, database upkeep, and live issue resolution.
